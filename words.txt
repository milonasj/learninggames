// Spelling words for Unicorn Rainbow Spelling
//
// How to add a new week:
//   1. Go to the bottom of this file.
//   2. Start the week with a line that begins with #, like:   # Week 4 (long a)
//   3. Put one word on each line. To have a sentence read aloud with it,
//      add a | after the word and then the sentence.
//   4. Save. The game picks up the new week the next time it opens.
//
// Lines that start with // (like these) are notes and are skipped.

# Week 1 (-ed words)
yelled | Mom yelled to us from the porch.
yanked | He yanked the rope as hard as he could.
slumped | She slumped on the couch after soccer.
limped | The puppy limped on its sore paw.
plopped | The frog plopped into the pond.
smiled | Grandpa smiled at the baby.
shrugged | He shrugged because he did not know.
liked | I liked the pizza at the party.
patted | She patted the cat on its head.
you | Thank you for the gift.

# Week 2 (-ing words)
smiling | The baby is smiling at me.
racing | The kids are racing to the swings.
hoping | I am hoping it snows tomorrow.
baking | Dad is baking cookies for the party.
inviting | She is inviting her whole class to her party.
confusing | This map is confusing to read.
tasting | We are tasting the soup to see if it needs salt.
competing | The two teams are competing for the trophy.
hopping | The bunny is hopping across the yard.
were | We were late for the bus.

# Week 3 (contractions)
wasn't | The dog wasn't hungry at lunch.
I'll | I'll help you clean your room.
you'll | You'll love this funny book.
she'll | She'll be home after soccer practice.
their | The kids put on their coats.
it's | It's time to go to bed.
that's | That's my favorite song!
she's | She's the fastest runner in our class.
isn't | The water isn't too cold today.
aren't | We aren't going to the park today.
