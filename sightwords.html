    Sight words

    One word per line. To add a sentence clue, put it after a colon, like got: I got a new toy. Words without a sentence are just said on their own.

    Fix how a word sounds

    If the voice says a word wrong, type how it should sound. The game still shows the real word, it only changes what the voice hears. Try a few spellings and tap 🔊 to hear each one. For “the,” try thuh.
